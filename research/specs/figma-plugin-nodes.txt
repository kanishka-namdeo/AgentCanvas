 In the Typings File , you will also find references to "mixin" interfaces. This is just a way to group related properties together. For example: pages, frames and groups can all have children, so they all have children-related properties such as .children
, .appendChild
.

 Our node properties can't be modeled using traditional object-oriented class hierarchy which is why we use the concept of mixins. The ChildrenMixin
 define the properties that all nodes with children have (e.g. append, insert) and FrameNode
s, PageNode
s, etc all compose ChildrenMixin
. However, a RectangleNode
 cannot have children and therefore does not inherit from ChildrenMixin
.
